Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect when you use the Vet Care mobile application (“App”), how we use and protect that information, and the choices available to you. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Developer / publisher: Metcon Hi-Tech
Contact: metconsoln@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Vet Care is built for veterinary professionals to manage field practice — appointments, customers, animals, medicines, and receipts. We handle your data with care and do not sell personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and hashed password when you sign up.
  • Clinic profile: Clinic name, doctor name, address, and phone number you provide for receipts.
  • Customer data: Names, phone numbers, and addresses of veterinary clients you enter.
  • Animal records: Species, breed, age, photos, and medical notes.
  • Appointment data: Dates, services, fees, prescriptions, and medicine records.
  • Device information: Device model and OS version for licensing and crash diagnosis.
  • Purchase / license data: Subscription or license status needed to unlock paid features (processed via Google Play / related billing services).

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the App’s core functionality.
  • To authenticate your account and validate your license.
  • To generate receipts and PDF reports.
  • To send local appointment reminders (reminders are scheduled on your device).
  • To sync data across your devices when you are signed in.
  • To improve reliability and diagnose issues.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your veterinary data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database where supported by the App. Cloud sync uses Supabase with Row-Level Security (RLS) so that only you can access your account data. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use:

  • Supabase — authentication, licensing, and optional cloud sync.
  • Google Play Services / Play Billing — app distribution, updates, and in-app purchases.

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data processing.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: Authentication, license verification, and optional sync.
  • Contacts: Optional — only when you choose to import a phone number.
  • Bluetooth: Optional — thermal receipt printers.
  • Notifications: Local appointment reminders.
  • Storage / files: Saving PDF receipts and related exports.

7. Data Retention

Data on your device remains until you delete the App or clear its data. Cloud-synced account data is retained while your account is active and is deleted when you request account deletion, subject to any short processing period needed to complete the request.

8. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Vet Care account and associated cloud data. See our dedicated page: Delete your Vet Care account.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
