Delete your Vet Care account

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This page is the official web resource for requesting deletion of your Vet Care app account and associated data (developer: Metcon Hi-Tech).

You can use this page even if you have already uninstalled the app. You do not need to reinstall Vet Care to submit a request.

Request account deletion by email

Tap the button below to open your email app with a pre-filled request. We will confirm and permanently delete your account and associated cloud data.

Email account deletion request Contact support

What will be deleted

  • Your Vet Care login / account
  • Cloud-synced clinic, customer, animal, appointment, and related records linked to that account
  • License / trial records tied to that account (where applicable)

What may remain temporarily

  • Data stored only on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear app data yourself.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play remain under Google’s systems and policies.
  • Limited records may be kept briefly if required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, then removed or anonymized.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to metconsoln@gmail.com with the subject Vet Care Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address you used to create your Vet Care account.
  3. We will verify ownership of the account and process the deletion.
  4. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete (typically within 7 days, often sooner).

Subscriptions

If you purchased a subscription through Google Play, cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions before or after requesting account deletion. Deleting your Vet Care account does not automatically cancel Google Play billing.
